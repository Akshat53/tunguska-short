## Studio settings checklist (both channels)
1. Settings > Channel > Basic info: language, country, keywords, description, links.
2. Settings > Channel > Advanced settings: **"No, set this channel as not made for kids"**.
   Cartoons get mis-flagged as kids' content, which switches off comments and hurts monetisation.
3. Settings > Upload defaults: add the description footer below, category **Education**, language.
4. Verify the channel by phone: youtube.com/verify.
5. Customization: upload avatar, banner, and add a channel trailer later.
6. Turn on 2-Step Verification on the Google account.
