In 1962, an epidemic shut down fourteen schools. The symptom? Laughter.
It started at a girls' school in Tanganyika. Three students began laughing… and couldn't stop. Soon, ninety-five had it.
They cried. They fainted. The fits lasted hours, then came back.
So officials closed the school and sent everyone home. Big mistake. The girls carried it to their villages.
Poison? Infection? Doctors found neither. So what was it? Tell me your theory.
The leading explanation: mass psychogenic illness. The country had won independence just weeks earlier, and these students lived under intense pressure. The stress came out as laughter.
It took over a year to fade. Around a thousand people. And there was never a joke.
If everyone around you started laughing right now… how long could you hold out?
