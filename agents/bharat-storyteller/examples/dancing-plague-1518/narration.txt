A crowd is dancing through Strasbourg, and no one can stop.
July, fifteen-eighteen.
What makes a city dance?
One woman started it, dancing alone in the street for days.
Within a week, dozens joined her.
The doctors' cure?
More dancing.
The council built a stage and hired musicians.
The crowd only grew.
Did anyone dance to death?
Later chronicles claim up to fifteen a day.
The city's own records mention none.
So why dance?
Strasbourg had suffered years of famine and disease.
And people feared Saint Vitus, who could curse sinners to dance.
The leading theory: mass psychogenic illness.
Fear, spreading body to body.
Would you have joined in?
Careful.
It only takes one dancer to start a whole crowd.
