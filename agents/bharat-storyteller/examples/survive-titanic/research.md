# Research: Could YOU Survive? Ep 1 — The Titanic (1912)
Research mode: web-verified (2026-11-04). Claims with ids and sources: research.json.

## Verified facts used
- C01 The Titanic sank at about 02:20 ship's time, 15 April 1912 (Wikipedia, Sinking of the Titanic; read).
- C02 About 1,500 died (range about 1,490-1,517) — description only.
- C03 20 lifeboats (16 wooden + 4 collapsible), room for about 1,178 — description only.
- C04 About 705-712 survived (Wikipedia gives 710; others 705-708/712) — description only, as a range.
- C05 Boats left partly empty: No. 7 about 27-28 of 65; No. 2, 25 of 40 (Wikipedia; Smithsonian snippet). Narration: "many half empty" (approximate).
- C06 Water about −2 °C / 28 °F: the widely reported figure (Cruise Hive, Encyclopedia Titanica snippets; some estimates differ). Narration: "about minus two degrees".
- C07 Cold shock: involuntary gasp + hyperventilation in roughly the first minute; a gasp under water can inhale water (CSBC 1-10-1; SIRIUSMEDx snippet).
- C08 1-10-1: ~1 min cold shock, ~10 min meaningful movement, ~1 h to unconsciousness; a memory aid, timings vary and shrink in colder water. Narration: "Ten minutes, maybe less".
- C09 Lifeboat 14 (Fifth Officer Harold Lowe) is described as the only lifeboat to go back and search; it pulled about four people from the water (one died). Some accounts count Lifeboat 4 (picked up swimmers close by) too.
- C10 Charles Joughin, chief baker, testified he was in the water "over two hours"; held on to Collapsible B, later taken into another boat. His own, uncorroborated account; historians think his head and upper body stayed dry-ish. Narration: "said he lasted about two hours".
- C11 Whisky: popular legend. In testimony Joughin spoke of "a drop of liqueur" (half a tumbler); he said he was not drunk (Maltin). Alcohol does not protect against cold water. Narration: "Legend says whisky. Scientists doubt it helped."
- C12-C14 (picture only): lights failed ~2:18 just before the plunge; the hull broke between funnels 3 and 4; calm, clear, moonless, starry night; the 4th funnel was a dummy.

## Disputed / qualified
- Water temperature (C06), survivor counts (C04), "only one boat" (C09), Joughin's two hours (C10).

## NOT USED (debunked)
- C15 "Joughin was blind drunk and that's why he survived" (Maltin: a myth).

## Unsure
- Whether Lowe's boat carried a lantern (shown for readability; plausible, not verified).
- Exact time the stern lights failed relative to 2:20 (shown flickering out ~5 s in).
