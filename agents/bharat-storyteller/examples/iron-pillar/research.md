## Subject in plain words
An iron column about 7.2 m long (about 6 m above ground), roughly six tonnes, stands in the courtyard of the Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque in the Qutb complex,
Mehrauli, Delhi. Its oldest inscription is a Sanskrit eulogy of a king "Chandra", generally identified as the Gupta emperor Chandragupta II, which calls
the pillar a *dhvaja* (standard) of Vishnu raised on a hill called Vishnupada. It was made by forge-welding iron lumps by hand. It is famous as the
"pillar that never rusts". It does rust: corrosion was fast at first, then a thin, compact protective film formed, catalysed by the unusually high
phosphorus in the iron (ancient smelting without lime left phosphorus in the metal), and helped by alternate wetting and drying. Indian viewers have
seen it on school trips; most believe the "rustless" version.

## Central question
If it is not magic iron, how has an open-air iron pillar survived about 1,600 years?
**Answer (revelation):** it rusts; the right kind of rust — a thin phosphate-rich layer — became its shield.

## Disputed interpretations
- **Identity of Chandra**: generally Chandragupta II; the inscription names no dynasty (C04) -> spoken as "माना जाता है".
- **Original location and mover** (Udayagiri / Vishnupadagiri; Anangpal Tomar vs Iltutmish): contested; left out (C24).
- **Cannon shooter**: no inscription or document records it; the 2009 paper's authors themselves defer attribution (C09-C11).
- **Numbers**: phosphorus 0.18-1% and film "1/20 mm" vary or are model-based (C22, C23): the script stays qualitative.
- **Environmental vs material theory**: CS 2000 credits material (phosphorus, slag) plus wet/dry cycles; dry climate alone is not the explanation (C26).

## Sensitivity notes
The pillar stands in the courtyard of the Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque and carries a Vaishnava inscription. The script describes neither faith's claims, avoids
any "who damaged it" blame (no record exists), and shows the smith as an anonymous craftsman with dignity.

## Open questions
Exact date the protective layer started (DTE says ~3 years; not used). Who moved the pillar to Delhi and when. Primary ASI monument page not reached this session.

## Hand-off: 3-5 most surprising verified facts
1. It does rust — fast at first (C14). 2. The protective layer is itself a phosphate-rich rust layer, catalysed by phosphorus (C15, C16).
3. The phosphorus is there because ancient smelters used no lime (C18). 4. After the 1997 cage, the hand-polished band turned dark brown within about a year (C20, C21).
5. A cannonball dented it without breaking it, and no record says who fired it (C08, C09).
