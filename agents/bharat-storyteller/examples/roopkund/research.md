# Research — Roopkund (Role 1)

**Research mode: web-verified** (WebSearch + WebFetch, 2026-11-01). Nature.com article page redirected to a login and was not read; the same paper was read in full text on PubMed Central.

## Subject summary
Roopkund is a small glacial lake at about 5,000 m in Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, on the route of the Nanda Devi Raj Jat pilgrimage. The scattered remains of several hundred people lie in and around it; a forest guard/ranger reported them in 1942. A 2004-05 investigation found injuries on top of some skulls, linked them to large hailstones, and radiocarbon dates around 850 CE suggested a single disaster, echoing a local folk song in which the goddess Nanda Devi strikes a royal procession with "iron balls" from the sky. In 2019 Harney et al. (Nature Communications) sequenced 38 individuals: 23 South Asian-related (7th-10th c. CE, perhaps several events), 14 closest to present-day Greeks/Cretans (around 1800 CE), 1 Southeast Asian-related. At least two events about 1,000 years apart. Why a Mediterranean group was there and how anyone died remain unexplained.

**Central question:** who were these people, and did they really die together?

## Disputed / conflicts
- Count of remains: 200 / 300+ / 500-600 / up to 800 depending on source → only "सैकड़ों".
- Altitude: 5029 m (paper), "over 5000 m" (MPI), Wikipedia lead 5,020 m vs infobox 4,536 m → "करीब पाँच हज़ार मीटर".
- Mediterranean group date: paper 17th-20th c.; MPI/NatGeo "around 1800" → "करीब अठारह सौ".
- Hail: an osteological report hypothesised hail; Harney et al. say other scenarios are plausible. Not ruled out, not proven.

## NOT USED (debunked / unsafe)
C18 single-hailstorm-for-everyone; C19 Japanese soldiers; exact death counts; "they were Greek" (genetics gives closest modern match, not nationality).

## Visual details
Small frozen tarn in a snow bowl; boulders and ridges; pale scattered shapes on the shore (draw only at distance); mist; a ridge trail used by the Raj Jat; storm clouds of the legend; lab screens; globe with two regions far apart.

## Sensitivity
Human remains of real people: no gore, no skull close-ups, no jokes. Nanda Devi is a living deity for local communities: the legend is described, not judged, and the goddess is not drawn. Do not imply the Mediterranean group were invaders or anything else unproven.

## Open questions
Why the eastern-Mediterranean group travelled to Roopkund; how either group died; whether more DNA (only 38 of hundreds sampled) would reveal further groups. No post-2019 study changing this was found in a 2026 search.
